## Beschreibung (zum Einfügen)

![Outfits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/01_outfits.jpg)

**BTB Skin Studio** brings what Bedrock players are used to into Java Edition: cosmetics, a character creator, emotes and skin packs – all in one wardrobe, fully in English and German.

Part of the **BTB Studios** mod family (*Behind The Blocks*).

## ✨ Features

### 👕 Wardrobe (key `J`)
- Over 50 own cosmetics in 7 categories: hats, crowns, glasses, backpacks, wings, capes, shoulder pets, effects and more
- Wear several at once; conflicts are solved automatically (a helmet replaces the hat, glasses stay)
- Colour them from a palette or freely, move and scale each one, save outfits
- Large 3D preview you can rotate, light and dark theme
- Your 3D character also appears in the **main menu and pause menu**, with a Wardrobe button below

![Wardrobe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/12_wardrobe_en.jpg)

### 🧍 Character creator – like Bedrock
- Pick a base skin, then choose **top, bottoms, shoes and jacket one by one** – 16 pieces such as flannel shirt, overalls, hoodie, jeans, boots, leather jacket – each in any colour
- **Body proportions:** height plus head, arm and leg thickness
- **Faces:** googly eyes, big eyes, sleepy eyes and blinking eyes with eyelids in your skin tone; smile, talking mouth and tongue-out with a dripping drop
- Character Creator pieces from your Bedrock installation show up as their own collection (if unencrypted)

![Character creator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/13_character_en.jpg)

### 💃 Emotes (key `B`)
- **20 emotes inspired by Minecraft itself** – wave, cheer, clap, facepalm, woodpunch, mining with a real diamond pickaxe and block particles, showing off diamonds, ghast dance, creeper scare, drinking a potion, eating a cookie, waving a banner, spyglass, dance, heart and more
- A real **emote wheel** with 5 slots you fill yourself, and a figure next to it that shows the emote
- Keys `1`–`5` in the wheel plus an optional direct key per slot; shown as keyboard key caps – or as controller buttons with **Controlify**
- Other players with the mod see your emotes

![Emotes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/02_emotes.jpg)
![Emote wheel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/15_wheel_en.jpg)

### 🎨 Skins and skin packs
- All standard skins (Steve, Alex, Ari, Efe, Kai, Makena, Noor, Sunny, Zuri – wide and slim)
- **BTB Skins:** our own pack with a lumberjack (Steve in a lumberjack outfit), adventurer, gardener, miner, sailor and astronaut
- **Bedrock skin packs:** loads the skin packs that come with your Bedrock installation, skin packs in resource packs, and `.zip`/`.mcpack` files in `skin_packs/`
- **Skin pack catalog:** download the classic Bedrock skin packs (birthday packs, Battle & Beasts, Biome Settlers, …) with one click
- **Find a Java player** by name and use their skin
- **Build your own skin pack** and export it as `.mcpack` – it works in Bedrock too
- **3D skin layers** like in Bedrock (hat, jacket, sleeves and pants as real voxels)

![Skins](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/16_skins_en.jpg)
![Catalog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/17_catalog_en.jpg)

### 🎃 Cosmetic packs
- **BTB Starter** – 38 cosmetics: torch hat with flame and glow, lily pad, glass terrarium backpack, crowns, halo, fire halo, fox mask, wings, cape, slime and mini dragon on your shoulder, fireflies …
- **Winter Magic** – bobble hat, scarf, earmuffs, antlers, snowflakes
- **Halloween** – glowing pumpkin head, witch hat, bat wings, ghost
- **Pirates** – tricorne, eye patch, parrot, cutlass
- Everything is animated – idle and while walking

### 🛡️ Armor-aware
With a helmet only glasses stay visible (on top of the helmet); everything else on the head is hidden. A chestplate moves backpacks behind it, an elytra hides wings, capes and backpacks. Nothing clips through armor.

![Back, face and armor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/03_back_face_armor.jpg)

### 🛠️ Cosmetic editor
- Import Blockbench models (Bedrock geometry) with texture and animation
- Attach to head, body, back, arms, legs or shoulders; move, rotate and scale with a live preview on your own player
- Animation presets (float, spin, sway, flap, pulse, flicker, wag) or Bedrock animations
- Tint masks, colour palettes, glow and particles
- Share as `.btbcosmetic` or whole packs as `.btbcosmeticpack` – every import is previewed and checked

![Editor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nik0bellic/BTB-Skin-Studio/feature/cosmetic-system/docs/modrinth/19_editor_en.jpg)

### 🌐 Multiplayer
Install the mod on the server and the clients: everyone sees each other's cosmetics, skins, emotes and body proportions. Your own cosmetics are sent once by content hash (a few hundred bytes for known ones). The server validates everything. Players without the mod are not affected.

## 📦 Installation
1. Install **Fabric Loader 0.20.1+** for Minecraft **26.3**
2. Put **Fabric API** and **BTB Skin Studio** into `mods`
3. For multiplayer, also put it on the server

## ⌨️ Controls
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `J` | Wardrobe |
| `B` | Emote wheel (`1`–`5` plays an emote, walking stops it) |
| Emote slot 1–5 | Direct keys, unbound by default |

Commands: `/skinstudio`, `/skinstudio editor`, `/skinstudio packs`, `/skinstudio equip <id>`, `/skinstudio clear`

## ℹ️ Good to know
- Encrypted Marketplace content is detected and **not** imported – no copy protection is bypassed.
- The mod ships no Mojang or Marketplace content. Standard skins and Bedrock packs come from your installed games; catalog packs are downloaded from their source only when you click.
- Player lookup only talks to Mojang's services and `textures.minecraft.net`.
